# 🎥 HerSafety - Hackathon Demo Script & Presentation

This script is designed for your 2-3 minute video submission required by the **RevenueCat Shipaton**, **Amazon Developer Hackathon**, and **First Commit** hackathons. 

---

## ⏱️ Structure (2-3 Minutes)
- **0:00 - 0:30** The Problem & The Solution
- **0:30 - 1:15** Core Features Demo (SOS & Guardian Grid)
- **1:15 - 1:45** AI & Monetization (SafeRoute AWS Bedrock & RevenueCat)
- **1:45 - 2:00** Alexa+ Voice Integration (Amazon Hackathon)
- **2:00 - 2:30** First Commit Learning & Conclusion

---

## 📝 The Script

### 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
*(Camera on you, smiling but serious)*
"Hi everyone, I'm Yash, and I built **HerSafety**. 
Women’s safety is a global crisis, but most safety tools on the market are either too expensive, too complicated, or require you to unlock your phone and navigate menus when every second counts. 

HerSafety changes that. It's a comprehensive mobile app designed to provide instant emergency assistance, AI-powered safe routing, and a trusted network of guardians—all wrapped in a beautifully fast UI."

### 2. Core Features Demo (0:30 - 1:15)
*(Screen recording of the app starts: Show Home Screen)*
"Let's look at the app. In a high-stress situation, you need clarity. The UI is designed to be calming but actionable. 

*(Click on SOS)*
If you are in danger, you tap the SOS button. 
*(Show the pulsing red ring animation)*
We built a custom React Native animation here with heavy haptic feedback. Once that timer hits zero, it bypasses silent mode and blasts your live location and audio recordings to your **Guardian Grid**—a pre-selected network of your trusted contacts."
*(Quickly show Guardian Grid screen)*

### 3. AI & Monetization (1:15 - 1:45)
*(Click on SafeRoute)*
"But safety is about prevention, too. That’s why I built **SafeRoute**. 
When you enter your destination, HerSafety doesn't just give you the shortest route. 

*(Show the scanner animation line going down the screen)*
We hit the **AWS Bedrock API using Claude 3 Sonnet** to analyze the route in real-time. It checks lighting data, historical crime rates, and foot traffic to give you an AI Safety Score. 

*(Show Paywall Screen)*
Because SOS is a fundamental human right, the core emergency features are always free. We use **RevenueCat** to handle our 'HerShield Pro' subscription, which unlocks these advanced AI routing features and our Live Threat Radar to fund the free tier."

### 4. Alexa+ Voice Integration (1:45 - 2:00)
*(Show terminal or diagram of MCP server)*
"For the Amazon Hackathon, I knew that sometimes you can't reach your phone. So, I built a custom **Alexa+ MCP Server**. 
You can simply say, *'Alexa, I am being followed'* and the MCP server intercepts the intent, automatically triggers the SOS protocol on your account, and alerts your guardians hands-free."

### 5. What I Learned & Outro (2:00 - 2:30)
*(Camera back on you)*
"As part of the **First Commit** hackathon, this was an incredible learning experience. I learned how to migrate a Next.js web concept into a native Expo mobile app, how to orchestrate complex React Native animations, and how to seamlessly integrate third-party SDKs like RevenueCat and AWS Bedrock.

HerSafety isn't just an app; it's a lifeline. Thank you to RevenueCat, Amazon, and Devpost for the opportunity to build something that matters."

## 🎬 Tips for Recording
1. **Screen Recording:** Use a phone simulator (like iOS Simulator) or mirror your physical phone to your screen.
2. **Audio:** Record your voice clearly in a quiet room.
3. **Lighting:** Make sure your face is well-lit when you are on camera. 
4. **Editing:** Use a free tool like CapCut or iMovie to splice your facecam with the screen recording.
